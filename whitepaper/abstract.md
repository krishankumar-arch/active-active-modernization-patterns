# Abstract: Active-Active Data Replication Architectures for Zero-Downtime Enterprise Modernization

**Author:** Krishan Kumar  
**Domain:** Enterprise Architecture & Distributed Systems  
**Focus:** Mission-Critical System Modernization and Management Information Systems (MIS)

## Research Summary
As global enterprises face the imperative to migrate complex Management Information Systems (MIS) to cloud-native environments, traditional migration strategies often present unacceptable risks to business continuity. This research proposes a **Live Data Coexistence Framework**—an active-active replication strategy designed to bridge the gap between mature monolithic environments and modern cloud ecosystems.

By architecting a resilient, tool-agnostic pipeline utilizing **Qlik Replicate** and **AWS Database Migration Service (DMS)**, this framework ensures real-time data synchronization with sub-second latency. The solution addresses fundamental distributed systems challenges, including **deterministic idempotency** and **chronological event sequencing**, ensuring that data—the lifeblood of the organization—remains consistent during long-term transformation journeys.

**Note:** The architectural patterns in this solution are universally applicable to data-synchronization challenges in any Management Information System (MIS) requiring high availability, rigorous data integrity, and zero-downtime modernization.
